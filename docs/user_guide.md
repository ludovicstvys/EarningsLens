# EarningsLens User Guide

![Dashboard screenshot](dashboard_red_demo.svg)

EarningsLens compares the latest quarter with the prior quarter and produces a short analyst-style brief plus four diagnostics. The banner at the top of the app is always a disclaimer: outputs are AI-generated analytical aids, not investment advice.

The headline signal is simple. `GREEN` means no major issues were flagged across sentiment, hedging, topics, and Q&A responsiveness. `AMBER` means one analytical dimension moved enough to warrant monitoring. `RED` means multiple independent stress signals appeared at once.

The Sentiment tab compares management's prepared remarks with the tone used once analysts start asking questions. A negative gap means the Q&A felt worse than the scripted opening. The Hedging tab tracks whether management used more uncertainty language such as "may," "could," or "pressure" than in the prior quarter. A sharp rise often signals weaker conviction.

The Topics tab shows whether management's prepared remarks stayed semantically close to the prior quarter and which themes were added or dropped. New risk-heavy themes are highlighted. The Risk vocab tab tracks finance-specific words like `inflation`, `liquidity`, `competition`, and `backlog`, surfacing the biggest movers first.

The Q&A Evasion Inspector focuses on whether executive answers actually addressed analyst questions. Low responsiveness scores do not prove misconduct; they indicate where a human reader should review the exchange closely.
